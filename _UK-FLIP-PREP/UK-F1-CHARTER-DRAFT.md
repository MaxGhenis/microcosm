# UK F1: drive the national and rowwise builds from bundle authority

**DRAFT — dispatch only after the US F1 certification and the UK P0 closure
gate below are both green.**

## Why this lane exists

US F1 supplies the certified generic executor, ambient brokers, seed broker,
comparison doctrine, and certification harness. UK F1 reuses those mechanisms
to make the UK bundle drive the national staging build, both rowwise geography
routes, and the constituency calibration candidate while leaving constants
mode runnable as the oracle.

The intended day-of exercise is a one-day country binding and evidence rerun,
not a second generic-executor project. On the inspected schema snapshot that
is **not yet possible**: typed UK sources/spine/geography normalize, but the
compiler does not turn those source stages into executable nodes; UK kernels
are contract-only; and compatibility gate/take-up/target/evidence resources
remain dropped `legacy_json`. The P0 package makes the one-day claim honest.

## What UK F1 is

- A generic-executor country binding over three published execution surfaces:
  national staging; rowwise geography (ladder and crosswalk); and rowwise
  constituency calibration.
- A stage-by-stage authority switch: constants mode constructs today's exact
  values; bundle mode constructs them from compiled IR and resolved run
  requests.
- Exact legacy behavior. Geography, clone identity, mass transitions, target
  formulas, gates, thresholds, seeds, QRF reset boundaries, filenames, and
  publish ordering must reproduce constants mode.
- A dual-mode, cold, restartable evidence exercise at fixture and 1% scale.
- A local-only lane. Commit each deliverable; do not push. The owner collects
  and adjudicates.

## What UK F1 is not

- Not a policy, data, model, solver, seed, threshold, vintage, geography, or
  release-name improvement. A bundle result that looks “better” but differs
  from the oracle is a defect.
- Not F2 deletion. Constants mode remains runnable until at least one full
  certified release and machine-decidable deletion criteria are satisfied.
- Not F3. Do not introduce block-first geography, new target families, new
  stochastic protocols, model retuning, or local-doctrine changes.
- Not permission to edit `policyengine-uk`, María's branches, or unrelated
  package sources.
- Not permission to admit the dormant firm generator into the selected UK
  pipeline. If scope expands, its authority and three ledger rows must be
  compiled and certified separately.
- Not permission to touch a production Logbook chain. Certification is
  off-chain; omit predecessor arguments and do not touch pending-chain files.

## Comparison doctrine

The US owner ruling's two-tier model applies:

1. **Normative vector — raw-byte exact, no normalization.** Entity H5 tables,
   every column and weight, stage checkpoint payloads, QRF/model banks,
   geography assignments, clone/source identities, calibration weights,
   target matrices, gate verdicts and rows, compiled seed map, and every
   plan-declared publication artifact must match.
2. **Provenance/operational vector — structural comparison under the sealed
   plan lock.** Generation/config-authority fields, allowed prefix differences
   if separately ruled, absolute paths, durations, nine operational nonce
   sites (seven UUID and two temp-name), 12 operational clock/timing sites,
   host/executable strings, and broker access logs each have an explicit rule.
   Any unlisted difference fails.
3. The injected weighted-integrity evaluation date is policy-significant and
   therefore equal in both modes, not operationally excluded.
4. `node_reuse_key` is byte-equal across modes and excludes provenance,
   operational nonces, and operational clocks. The injected policy evaluation
   date is a behavior-relevant run input and enters every affected gate node's
   reuse key.
