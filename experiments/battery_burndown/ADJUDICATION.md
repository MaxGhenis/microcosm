# US f025 release-battery adjudication

## Decision

The baseline battery contains **93 red comparator checks covering 60 physical legs**. Every check is enumerated below. The actionable disposition is **55 FIX-CANDIDATE, 2 REVIEWED-EXCLUSION-CANDIDATE, and 36 BLOCKER**. At the physical-leg level, those counts are 35, 2, and 23 respectively.

This is analysis only. It does not change a build, artifact, comparator band, gate, threshold, or publication state. No isolated arm is a landable remedy: eligibility greens eight baseline checks but introduces eleven others; investment introduces four and greens none; order changes no failure membership.

## Counting and scope

The apparent roughly 93 red legs are 93 failed criteria. They consolidate to 60 physical leg IDs because 33 physical legs fail both incidence and quantile shape. This report therefore keys each adjudication as `physical_leg#criterion` so every terminal failure is represented exactly once. The baseline split is 43 incidence, 48 quantile-envelope, and 2 dead-comparison checks; there are no red categorical checks.

The comparator appends criterion failures and returns `passed=not failures` (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11744-11748`; `packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11819-11856`; `packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11860-11930`; `packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11990-12021`). The pool invokes the gates, serializes `result.terminal_gates` through `GateReport`, and writes the same object under `terminal_gates` and the compatibility alias `agreement_gate` (`tools/build_us_multispine_pool.py:3264-3274,3383-3384,3710-3720,3731`); `GateReport` retains every named result and its complete failures (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/gates.py:180-203,299-309`). Consequently, the mirrored `agreement_gate.gates` payload is not counted a second time.

### Classification rule

- **FIX-CANDIDATE** means the evidence identifies a real measured artifact mismatch and names a concrete calibration, reconciliation, or targeted-model change.

- **REVIEWED-EXCLUSION-CANDIDATE** means a possible measurement-scope mismatch, not a waiver. It is conditional on independent evidence and authenticated criterion-level machinery. Any real artifact defect remains a fix or blocker.

- **BLOCKER** means the red check requires named data, ownership, dense validation, label, or model work before a defensible fix can be chosen.

## Comparator mechanisms

- The f025 comparator freezes an inclusive incidence-ratio band of [0.80, 1.25], a QED ceiling of 0.25, and p10/p25/p50/p75/p90 weighted quantiles (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11305-11309`).

- Scope is rebuilt at positive weight within clone role: clone-0/native rows are compared between the ASEC and ACS origin channels (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11523-11580`).

- Incidence is `sum(weight on nonzero/sign leg) / sum(weight in that origin scope)`; the tested ratio is ACS incidence divided by ASEC incidence and is compared inclusively with the frozen band. Boolean legs additionally fail as dead when both weighted incidences are zero (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11819-11856,11976-11987`). Monetary legs are split by sign, so each sign can independently fail incidence and magnitude (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11860-11930`).

- QED is `max_q 2*|q_ASEC-q_ACS|/(|q_ASEC|+|q_ACS|)` over weighted inverse-ECDF p10/p25/p50/p75/p90 carrier quantiles (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11918-11930,11990-12021`). The literal support floor is five (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:3026-3030`): fewer than five positive-weight scoped rows on either origin makes the whole comparison untestable (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11629-11640,11716-11724`), while after that check monetary incidence runs and fewer than five carriers on either sign skips only that sign's QED (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11912-11917`).

- The transfer path writes decoded QRF draws into missing target cells without a separate post-draw marginal-calibration stage (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/acs_transfer.py:1003-1071,1100-1227,1581-1648,1692-1703,2951-3035`; `tools/build_us_multispine_pool.py:3374-3379`). The fitter distinguishes degenerate, single-sign, and gated regimes: only the gated zero-inflated/sign-mixed path fits a sample-weighted sign gate, samples it, and then draws sign-conditional magnitudes (`packages/microcosm-fit/src/microcosm/fit/qrf.py:950-1003,1333-1380,1389-1429`). The table therefore proposes calibration of existing gate probabilities only for applicable carrier/sign mismatches rather than adding a nonexistent hurdle, and distinguishes calibratable marginal defects from rare-tail, coupled-identity, and label/data blockers.

## Reviewed-exclusion finding

**The current US f025 by-origin battery does not support reviewed exclusions.** Its API accepts comparison inputs and emits failures without an exclusion register (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11366-11378`), and the pool invokes it directly (`tools/build_us_multispine_pool.py:3264-3274`). Copying a UK exclusions JSON into the tree would have no effect on this gate. The by-origin battery does have deterministic structural-absence scoping, but that path requires an exact declared absence equation and emits a failure when it does not reconcile (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11545-11576`); it is authority-bound scope, not a reviewed defect exception.

By contrast, the UK terminal stack declares reviewed resources in its gates contract (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk/gates.json:37-44,183-207`). The UK driver conditionally loads a committed default or supplied JSON override into typed input-mass and QRF-tail policies, refuses partially armed configurations, preflights the degenerate default/override, and passes the policies into the build (`tools/build_uk_national_dataset.py:385-443,821-834,901-922`); the build inserts them into the terminal gate evidence context (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk_runtime/national_build.py:521-540`). The common loader rejects invalid schema-2 JSON and enforces the exact `reason`, `approved_by`, `adjudication`, `approved_on`, and `expires_on` receipt fields (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk_runtime/weighted_integrity.py:182-321,405-477`). Runtime bindings verify configured resource names, resolve the degenerate register, and pass typed policies to their gates (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk_runtime/battery_bindings.py:219-286,379-437`). The input-mass wrapper applies the resolved policy, probes stale/dormant entries, and enforces receipt dates; the QRF-tail wrapper enforces receipt dates while the shared tail gate implements used/stale/dormant semantics (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk_runtime/weighted_integrity.py:752-841,911-1019`; `packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/gates.py:1152-1279,2505-2648`). The degenerate gate separately applies in-force exclusions and reports stale, expired, premature, and dormant entries (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk_runtime/terminal_gates.py:918-1056`). This precedent has an attestation limitation: the degenerate binding exposes full resolved records through its evidence hook, the input-mass binding evidence-hashes only its reference, and the tail binding declares no evidence hook (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk_runtime/battery_bindings.py:271-286,410-416,477-482,519-540`). Thus the loader and gates validate and evaluate all five receipt fields, but input-mass/QRF-tail receipts are not uniformly surfaced in binding evidence like degenerate receipts. Today the QRF-tail and input-mass registers are empty, and the sole live UK schema-2 exclusion is `household.source_year` in the degenerate register (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk/qrf_tail_reviewed_exclusions.json:1-5`; `packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk/input_mass_reviewed_exclusions.json:1-5`; `packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/uk/degenerate_reviewed_exclusions.json:1-13`). The US candidates therefore borrow a machinery precedent, not an existing UK tail/mass waiver.

The only two candidates here are the dead clone-0/native QBI booleans. The canonical battery declares both variables and forces every declared target to clone 0 (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:2867-2871,2964-2970`). They are **measurement-mismatch candidates** because operator-role provenance maps clone 0 to the ASEC-compatible native role and the primary clone-1 donor detail to the PUF-compatible role, regardless of ASEC/ACS origin (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/support_provenance.py:34,370-473,476-491`), while QBI reconciliation forces and writes the native SSTB flags false on both origins (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/qbi_inputs.py:1246-1264,1299-1305,1360-1363`). Approval would be honest only after new clone-role-scoped evidence proves that clone-1 PUF-role signal and coupled identities are live. An available upstream whole-frame gate computes weighted signal shares and coupled identities, but it is not one of this pool's two f025 terminal gates and does not supply clone-role/origin-scoped proof (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/qbi_inputs.py:1377-1405,1450-1533`; `tools/build_us_multispine_pool.py:3264-3274`). Otherwise the two legs are BLOCKERs. This battery would first need a schema-2 exact leg-plus-criterion register, the same five receipt fields and date validation, anti-rot checks, and fully sealed resolved receipts in the terminal manifest.

There are **zero sparse-artifact candidates**. Across the 48 red QEDs, the minimum carrier counts are 48 on ASEC and 519 on ACS, both above the literal five-carrier support profile (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:3026-3030`) and its QED-only skip (`packages/microcosm-build/src/microcosm/build/us_runtime/stacked_spine.py:11912-11917`). The one-sided Keogh leg has 2 ASEC rows and 0 ACS rows, so QED is skipped but incidence still fails. It is policy-relevant and remains a real blocker.

1. **PUF-tax regime-gate probability plus conditional-quantile calibration** — 38 FIX-CANDIDATE checks. Calibrates existing sign/carrier selection separately from conditional amount shape for the largest coherent defect cluster.
2. **Baseline retirement audit, dense regime-gated refit, and bundle-factorial guardrail** — 16 BLOCKER checks. Diagnoses the already-red baseline source/label/carrier/amount surface on its own evidence; separately isolates the bundle-only predictor replacement before it can land.
3. **Source-operator two-part carrier and amount calibration** — 13 FIX-CANDIDATE checks. Calibrates carrier prevalence and conditional amounts while retaining source reconciliation.
4. **Establish QBI ownership, refit the coupled surface, and adjudicate clone scope** — 8 BLOCKER plus 2 conditional REVIEWED-EXCLUSION-CANDIDATE checks. The refit and the clone-scope/US-exclusion decision are independent lanes; neither can substitute for missing ownership or identity evidence.
5. **Rare signed-tail donor evidence and target-specific modeling** — 9 BLOCKER checks. Covers the collectibles, alimony, casualty, farm-loss, and prior-year self-employment-loss tails that need denser evidence before refitting.

Separate release-risk priority: audit rare health-coverage labels and fit calibrated classifiers for the three coverage blockers. CHAMPVA remains outside the band under every arm and should not be mistaken for an arm-resolved leg.
