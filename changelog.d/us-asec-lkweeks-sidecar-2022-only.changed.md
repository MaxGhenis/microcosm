The US base builder reads the income-2022 `LKWEEKS` sidecar (`asecpub23csv.zip`) only when income year 2022 is pooled, or for `--base-h5` builds, and refuses `--asec-2023-weeks-unemployed-source` for a pooled build without 2022. For such a pool, including the 2023-2025 default, the raw-stage `LKWEEKS` mapping now pins each pooled year's Census person member instead of the unused sidecar. It records, per year, the H5 digest the values came from and the rows verified, and its exact `(source_year, PERIDNUM)` join verifies every carried value and fills none. `load_asec_raw_stage_checkpoint` refuses an `LKWEEKS` mapping that does not match the pooled years, including the sidecar pin the builder previously wrote for pools without 2022. Pools with 2022 keep byte-identical records.
