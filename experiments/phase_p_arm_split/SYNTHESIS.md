## Attribution supported by the evidence

### Retirement cluster

Order and investment are exactly unchanged at 114.63718321141073 `O` and 114.63718321141073 `I`. Eligibility reaches 113.23606657519205 `E`, delta -1.4011166362186742 `E` with CI [-3.2000114150660317 `E`, 0.2620452172778749 `E`], so the three-column eligibility arm does not reproduce the harm.

For that target, the bundle's resolved predictor set removes `__acs_transfer_social_security_income` and `__acs_transfer_retirement_income` while adding 14 `R1` broader predictors. Eligibility retains both direct income predictors and adds only hearing, seeing, and Medicare. Loss of the direct income predictors is the leading hypothesis suggested by this contrast, but it is not isolated from the simultaneous additions or bundle interactions.

The non-additivity extends to the cluster: the bundle significantly worsens social-security disability, dependents, survivors, taxable IRA, taxable private pension, tax-exempt private pension, and taxable SEP rows shown in the money table. Isolated arms do not reproduce those magnitudes; investment makes tax-exempt private pension significantly better rather than worse.
